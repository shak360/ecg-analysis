Runs entirely on this device

Not a medical device. This tool assists visual comparison of ECG tracings for research and educational use. It currently reports measurements with their uncertainty. Gain is read from the calibration pulse where one is printed, and assumed otherwise — the table says which. A change is flagged only when it exceeds both the detection limit of that pair and the clinical threshold. All findings must be verified against the original tracings by a qualified clinician.
