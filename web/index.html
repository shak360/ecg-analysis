Runs entirely on this device

Not a medical device. This tool assists visual comparison of ECG tracings for research and educational use. Measurements are derived from digitized images and carry uncertainty that depends on source quality. Suggested labels are automated pattern-matching against published criteria — a starting point for your own reading, never a diagnosis. They are applied only when you accept them, they omit conditions this tool cannot assess, and an absent suggestion means nothing. All findings must be verified against the original tracings by a qualified clinician.
